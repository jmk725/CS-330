Project Reflection
How do I approach designing software?

I approach software design by first understanding the requirements and breaking the problem into smaller, manageable parts. For this project, I focused on identifying the key components of the 3D scene—objects, transformations, textures, lighting, and camera behavior—before writing code. This helped me stay organized and avoid being overwhelmed by the complexity of the project.

What new design skills has my work on the project helped me to craft?

This project helped me develop stronger skills in visual design and spatial thinking. I learned how to design a scene with intention, considering object placement, scale, and perspective to create a cohesive environment. I also improved my ability to think about how lighting and textures affect the overall look and realism of a scene.

What design process did I follow for my project work?

I followed an iterative design process. I started with a basic scene layout, then gradually added objects, textures, and lighting. As I progressed, I tested frequently and made adjustments based on visual results and feedback from earlier milestones. This process allowed me to refine the scene step by step.

How could tactics from my design approach be applied in future work?

Breaking complex problems into smaller pieces and testing often can be applied to any future software project. This approach helps catch issues early and makes large projects more manageable, whether I am working on graphics, applications, or other software systems.

How do I approach developing programs?

When developing programs, I focus on building functionality incrementally. I prefer to start with a working foundation and then add features one at a time. This helps ensure that the program remains stable and easier to debug.

What new development strategies did I use while working on my 3D scene?

I relied heavily on experimentation and debugging, especially when working with lighting, which was the most challenging part of the project. I learned to adjust shaders, light positions, and normals while observing how small changes affected the scene.

How did iteration factor into my development?

Iteration was a key part of my development process. I repeatedly tested my scene, identified issues, made adjustments, and tested again. This was especially important for lighting and transformations, where trial and error helped me understand how different values impacted the final result.

How has my approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?

Over the course of the milestones, I became more comfortable reading existing code, making targeted changes, and troubleshooting issues independently. I also gained confidence in using debugging techniques instead of starting over when something did not work.

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science helps me build strong problem-solving and analytical skills that are useful in many professional settings. It also gives me a better understanding of how software systems work, which is valuable in both technical and leadership roles.

How do computational graphics and visualizations give me new knowledge and skills?

This course gave me hands-on experience with graphics programming, shaders, transformations, and rendering pipelines. These skills help me better understand how visual systems work and how complex software systems are built.

How can these skills be applied in my future educational or professional pathway?

The skills I developed in computational graphics can be applied to future coursework, game development, simulations, user-interface design, and other visualization-based projects. They also strengthen my overall confidence in learning and applying new technical concepts.
